# Agensi listing: Student Budget Planner

**Title (≤ 60):** Student Budget Planner: Stipend, Rent & Currency

**Summary (≤ 300):**
Free budget planner for students and young professionals. Turns a stipend, part-time pay and rent in any currency into a simple monthly plan with a currency cushion, inflation check, emergency fund, a plan for months without stipend and a spreadsheet layout. Not financial advice.

**Description (≤ 1024):**
A simple, honest monthly money plan for students and young professionals, in your language.

What it does:
- Takes your stipend, part-time or freelance pay, family support, rent and costs in any currency.
- Builds one plan in your home currency: needs, savings, wants and what is left, plus a weekly spending limit.
- Converts currencies with the official central-bank rate (with date) or your own rate, and adds a currency cushion.
- Plans the hard months: summer without stipend, a late first payment, residence-permit fees, a flight home.
- Sets an emergency-fund target and shows how long it takes.
- Irregular income: plans on your low month; tax savings with official tax-office links only.
- Gives a copy-ready Excel / Google Sheets layout.

Where it stops: educational only, not financial advice. No investments, loans or banks recommended. No tax or legal figures from memory. Never asks for logins, card or ID numbers.

**Categories:** Finance & Accounting (first) · Education & Learning · Productivity

**Tags:** budgeting, personal-finance, students, international-students, scholarships, young-professionals, emergency-fund, currency, multilingual, free

**Use cases:**
1. Build a monthly budget from a scholarship stipend and rent abroad
2. Plan when the stipend and rent are in different currencies
3. Find a safe weekly spending limit for food and transport
4. Plan for summer months without stipend
5. Set an emergency-fund target and timeline
6. Budget irregular part-time or freelance income and save for tax
7. Get a copy-ready Excel or Google Sheets budget layout
8. Check a budget for gaps before moving abroad

**Compatibility note:** Works on any agent. Official exchange rates, inflation and tax pages need web search; without it, the skill uses your numbers or labelled assumptions.

**Plan compatibility:** works on Free, Pro, Max and Team plans. Without web search it gives no rates from memory: paste the official rate or use your own. Short on messages? Say so and it answers one part at a time.

**Needs access to:** web search / web fetch (optional).

**Limitations / FAQ:**
- Is this financial advice? No. It organises your own numbers.
- Does it know my tax rate? It links the official tax office and tells you to verify with an accountant; it gives no rate from memory.
- Does it connect to my bank? No. Type or paste amounts (without account numbers).

**Demo ("See it in action")**: use the blind-eval answer to case 2 in `tests/` (Budapest stipend in EUR + family money in USD, summer gap).
