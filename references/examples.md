# Worked examples (fictional people and numbers)

All amounts are invented for the example. They are not official stipend, rent or tax figures.

## 1. Student in Baku, stipend + irregular part-time (AZN)
Input: stipend 150; café job paid 280, 320, 300 in the last 3 months; shared rent 200; food 120; metro 30; phone 15; wants a laptop fund.
- Income: plan on the **lowest** job month → 150 + 280 = **430**
- Needs: 200 + 120 + 30 + 15 = **365**
- Available: 430 − 365 = **65** → Emergency 30 · Laptop fund 20 · Wants 15 → **Left 0**
- Weekly limit: (120 + 30 + 15) / 4.3 ≈ **38.37**
- Emergency fund: 1 month of needs = 365 → at 30/month ≈ 12.2 → **13 months**; 3 months = 1,095
- Good month (job pays 320): the extra 40 goes to the emergency fund first.
- Tax line (only if the job is freelance/self-employed): "Check how this income is taxed at taxes.gov.az (195-1). Verify with an accountant."

## 2. Student abroad, stipend in EUR + family money in USD
Input: stipend 600 EUR paid Sep–Jun (10 months, none in Jul–Aug); family sends 200 USD/month all year; user's rate 1 USD = 0.90 EUR (your rate); rent 350; food 200; transport pass 25; phone 10; residence-permit renewal 60 in March; flight home 250 in July.
- Family money: 200 × 0.90 = 180 EUR; cushion 5% → 180 × 0.95 = **171**
- Income (study months): 600 + 171 = **771**
- Needs: 350 + 200 + 25 + 10 = **585**
- Summer (Jul–Aug): needs 2 × 585 = 1,170 − family 2 × 171 = 342 → gap **828**
- Sinking fund total: 828 + flight 250 + permit 60 = **1,138** ÷ 10 study months ≈ **113.80/month**
- Available: 771 − 585 = 186 → Sinking 114 · Emergency 40 · Wants 32 → **Left 0**
- Weekly limit: (200 + 25 + 32) / 4.3 ≈ **59.77**
- Emergency fund: 585 at 40/month ≈ 14.6 → **15 months** to 1 month of needs.
- Note: if the first stipend arrives late, the sinking fund isn't built yet → ask family for one month up front or ask the university about an advance (verify).

## 3. Gap month (Left < 0)
Needs 520, income 480 → Left −40. Options with numbers: cancel a 10 streaming subscription (−10); cook 3 more days a week (≈ −25, user's estimate); 2 extra work hours a week at 6/h ≈ 51/month if the visa allows (verify the official limit). Never: a loan, credit card or "investment".
